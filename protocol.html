PROTOCOL

2023

Protocol is an audiovisual object dedicated to complex digital protocols used in our everyday life.

The object consists of two separate devices communicating to each other with the use of light.

Devices are generating data sets based on received information. Optic fiber is used to transfer light signals.

Bottom device includes circuits for sound synthesis that are triggered in accordance with light signals. It also has CV inputs/outputs for communication with modular systems.
