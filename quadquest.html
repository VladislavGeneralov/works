quadquest

quadquest is a minimalistic handmade 2d game.

The main aim is to stay alive and to kill as much alien creatures as you can.

You have to harvest energy to shoot.

This game was created using the Processing programming environment with a little help from chatGPT.

quadquest was exposed to visitors of one of the events from kultura team (Pavlodar).

Specially for this event a custom USB joystick was built.
