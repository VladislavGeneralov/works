klad mineralov @ ŞUAQ festival (15.08.2026 / Charyn Canyon)

2026

klad mineralov's live performance on the second episode of ŞUAQ Festival in 2026.
ŞUAQ took place in Charyn Canyon National Park, and I performed just an hour after sunset.

I created a custom software system 'Moon Phases', synthesising ambient pads and rhythmic structures, slicing field recordings and making unpredictable choices. I used my MIDI controller to control some parameters of the system, and as additional audio channels I also used my programming workstation and a couple of walkie-talkies.

Unfortunately, there is no video documentation of the performance.
