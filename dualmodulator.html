VLADISLAV GENERALOV

Dual Delay / Frequency Modulator

This device can be used to transform any input audio signal by frequency modulations and delays. It has its special ‘craziness’ mode that randomly modulates delay times at random timing.

The device is powered by ‘Daisy Seed’ board by Electrosmith with a PlugData patch as a firmware.
