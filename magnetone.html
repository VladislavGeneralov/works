MAGNETONE

Magnetone is a sound object exploring magnetic and electromagnetic physical effects.

Five elements with permanent magnets and electromagnetic coils make up Newton's magnetic pendulum. Those coils pick up electromagnetic signals produced by five analog generators placed underneath.

Before prototyping Magnetone object, I made some experiments and explorations with DIY electromagnetic sensor.
