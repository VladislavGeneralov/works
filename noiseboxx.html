VLADISLAV GENERALOV

NOISEBOXX

2023

This device is an electroacoustic instrument with 3 springs and a fidget spinner.

It has a lo-fi looper and a delay module. Delay speed can be modulated with light.

So fidget spinner is used not only to create acoustic effects but to modulate delay speed.
